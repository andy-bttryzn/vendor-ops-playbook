# Vendor Ops Playbook

An operating manual for running vendor-heavy operations (lead-gen, channel partnerships, marketplace, anywhere with 50+ counterparties pushing email, contracts, returns, and money through the same human).

This is the document you give to the next person who's going to do the job — or to an AI assistant you're wiring into your workflow.

## Who this is for

- An ops lead at a 10–200-person company with more counterparties than dedicated CSMs
- A solo operator running multiple vendor relationships out of one inbox
- A consultant standing up vendor-ops infrastructure for a client
- An AI assistant being onboarded to do this work alongside (or under) a human

If your day involves answering the question *"what's the state of this vendor"* fifty times a week, this is for you.

## How to use this

**Sequential read:** chapters 0–3 give you the conceptual frame. Chapters 4–8 are the working mechanics. Chapters 9–11 are operating discipline that wraps everything.

**Reference read:** each chapter is standalone. Open the one you need. Cross-references inside are linked.

**Adoption arc:** start with chapter 1 (the model). Layer in chapter 5 (labels). Then 3 (brief) and 6 (tasks). The rest layers on as you hit the failure modes that justify them.

## What this isn't

- Not a SaaS pitch. You can implement everything here in Gmail + a spreadsheet + a willingness to be opinionated about your own workflow.
- Not a sales methodology. Vendor-ops sits behind sales; this is about running the relationships sales already opened.
- Not framework-neutral. Every recommendation here came from a specific failure mode. The shapes that survived are opinionated for a reason.
